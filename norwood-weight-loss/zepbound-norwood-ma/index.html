    How Zepbound Treatment Works in Norwood, MA

    How Zepbound Works in Norwood

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. By activating both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, it slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps the body manage blood sugar after eating. Most people feel full on smaller portions. It is injected under the skin of the stomach, thigh or upper arm once a week.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. It is also approved for adults with a BMI of 27 or higher who have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea. A separate approval covers moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in Norwood

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. The 2.5 mg dose is a starter dose meant to help your body adjust, and it is not where most of the effect comes from. From there, doses climb in 2.5 mg steps every four weeks or more, through 5, 7.5, 10 and 12.5 mg to a top of 15 mg. Maintenance doses are typically 5, 10 or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in Norwood

    The first month is mostly quiet. You may notice a smaller appetite within the first week, and stomach upset tends to cluster in the day or two after a shot or a dose increase. The SURMOUNT-1 clinical study reported average weight loss of roughly 20 percent at the 15 mg dose over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Other common effects include constipation, heartburn, belly pain and mild injection site reactions, and they usually fade as your body adjusts. Rarer and more serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones, dehydration that harms the kidneys and allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in Norwood

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. Next comes your medical history and every medication you take, with special attention to insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills. Anyone with medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in their family is not a candidate, and Zepbound is not used while pregnant. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in Norwood

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. Zepbound carries the weight management label and Mounjaro carries the type 2 diabetes label, which matters for insurance and for how your provider writes the prescription. In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss than Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide comes from compounding pharmacies and is not FDA-approved.
