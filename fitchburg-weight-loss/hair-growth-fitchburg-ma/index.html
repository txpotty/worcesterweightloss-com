    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Fitchburg, MA

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Fitchburg

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. It is driven by DHT, a hormone made from testosterone that slowly shrinks scalp follicles in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, which is why it follows a predictable pattern at the temples and crown. The two main medicines are finasteride, which cuts DHT, and minoxidil, which lengthens the growth phase. Combined use tends to work better than one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause, but thyroid problems, low iron, a recent pregnancy, crash dieting and major stress can all cause shedding that looks similar. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Fitchburg

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Topical minoxidil extends the growth phase and thickens individual hairs. The pairing is the standard. A compounded combination spray is another route, though it is not FDA-approved.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. Approved for prostate enlargement, it is used off-label for hair, usually after finasteride falls short. It lingers. Because it stays in the body for months, providers screen more carefully. Minoxidil rounds it out.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Fitchburg

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. It is not FDA-approved. Women and finasteride-averse men use it most.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Men with typical pattern loss often start on finasteride and minoxidil. Women usually start with minoxidil.

    What to Expect Over Time in Fitchburg

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. That is why most treatments take three to six months to show a visible change and around twelve months to reach their full effect, even when they are working from the first week. Early shedding on minoxidil is common and usually temporary. Stopping treatment lets the loss resume.
