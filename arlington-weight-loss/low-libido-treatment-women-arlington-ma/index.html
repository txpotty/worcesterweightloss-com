    How Low Libido Treatment for Women Works in Arlington, MA

    What Causes Low Libido in Arlington

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Common contributors include SSRIs and SNRIs, hormonal birth control, perimenopause, thyroid problems, chronic stress, poor sleep, pain during sex and relationship strain. So does how you feel in your body. Persistent, distressing low desire without another explanation is called hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. It often helps women whose interest changed after starting a medication, having a baby, entering perimenopause or going through a stressful stretch at work or home. No diagnosis is needed first. Women who are simply not bothered by lower desire do not need treatment, since HSDD is defined partly by distress. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Arlington

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. PT-141, brand name Vyleesi, is a 1.75 mg self-injection taken at least 45 minutes before sex, with limits of once a day and eight times a month. Oxytocin is a compounded option. It comes as a nasal spray or troche before intimacy, used off-label and without FDA approval. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 can work with the first dose. Flibanserin takes four to eight weeks, and it is typically stopped at the eight week mark if there is no benefit. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Arlington

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Alcohol and certain interacting drugs make them stronger, which is why it is taken at bedtime with drinking limits. PT-141 often causes nausea. Flushing, headache, injection site redness, a short blood pressure bump and skin darkening with frequent use are also possible. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. It is also avoided with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as fluconazole, clarithromycin or some HIV medications, and alcohol must be limited near bedtime. PT-141 has different limits. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Arlington

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It tends to suit women who rarely drink and prefer one routine pill, though the average benefit in trials was modest, around one extra satisfying sexual event per month compared with placebo. PT-141 is as-needed. That suits some women, but nausea puts others off. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
