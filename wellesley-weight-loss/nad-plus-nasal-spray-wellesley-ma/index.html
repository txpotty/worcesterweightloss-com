    How NAD+ Nasal Spray Works in Wellesley

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to convert nutrients into ATP, and it supports enzymes involved in DNA repair, with levels that tend to decline as people age. The spray puts NAD+ on the nasal lining. Part of each spray is absorbed through the vessel-rich lining, skipping digestion, but the total is lower than with injections and brain delivery remains uncertain.

    Who NAD+ Nasal Spray Is Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, focus and healthy aging who prefer to avoid needles are the usual candidates. It suits people who want a low-commitment trial of NAD+ before trying shots, or a lighter maintenance routine after finishing an injection course. It does not treat disease. People who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or ongoing sinus disease may not be candidates.

    NAD+ Nasal Spray Dosing and Schedule in Wellesley

    Once or twice daily, mornings first. Many protocols begin with a single spray per nostril in the morning, then increase the count or add a midday dose after a week or two. Late doses may keep you up. Store it cold, and prime the pump into the air before the first use or after a few days off.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Expect some stinging early. A mild burn or an odd taste at the back of the throat is common, and some people report a gentle lift in energy or focus within one to two weeks while others notice little. Effects tend to be mild. Your provider adjusts the count at check-ins.

    NAD+ Nasal Spray Side Effects in Wellesley

    Nasal irritation is the most common. Expect some sneezing, a drippy nose or a bitter taste, and a few people get headaches or a brief flush. Occasional nosebleeds show up in dry climates or with heavy use, and pointing the spray toward the outer wall of the nostril plus a bedroom humidifier lowers the risk. Report persistent bleeding.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and nasal health first. Expect questions on pregnancy, cancer, sinus infections, polyps, nosebleeds and the names of any other nasal sprays in your cabinet. Bloodwork is uncommon here. List supplements like NMN too.

    NAD+ Nasal Spray Compared to Injections in Wellesley

    Injections deliver more. A shot under the skin gives a bigger and steadier dose, while an IV drip gives the most and costs an afternoon in a chair. The spray trades dose for comfort. NMN and NR supplements are precursors rather than NAD+ itself.
