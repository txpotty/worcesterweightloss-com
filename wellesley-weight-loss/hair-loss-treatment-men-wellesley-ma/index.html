    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Wellesley, MA

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Wellesley

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. DHT is a hormone made from testosterone, and in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, DHT slowly shrinks follicles at the temples and crown until they make thin, short hairs or none at all. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil, on its own path, helps follicles grow longer and thicker.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. Results are strongest where some hair remains, since long-bald areas seldom regrow. Earlier is better. Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss needs an in-person exam instead. Your photos confirm the pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Wellesley

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Minoxidil comes as a topical used once or twice a day, or as a low-dose off-label tablet. A compounded combination spray is applied once a day. Dutasteride is a capsule taken daily.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. By month six, loss typically slows and density starts to improve. Fuller results show between nine and twelve months.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Wellesley

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. These usually resolve after stopping, and many men who notice them find they fade even while staying on the medicine. Rarely, men report low mood, which should be reported right away. Scalp itch is the usual topical complaint. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. Expect questions about mood history, libido and family planning, because each can change the plan. Finasteride lowers PSA results by about half. Mention it at your next prostate screening.

    How Men's Options Compare in Wellesley

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. Dutasteride with minoxidil is a stronger DHT-blocking option, used off-label, for men who did not get enough from finasteride after about a year. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a topical-only blend for men who want to skip DHT blockers. Surgery is an option for some.
