    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Needham, MA

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Needham

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. It is driven by DHT, a hormone made from testosterone that slowly shrinks scalp follicles in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, which is why it follows a predictable pattern at the temples and crown. Finasteride lowers DHT, and minoxidil supports growth directly. Combined use tends to work better than one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause, but thyroid problems, low iron, a recent pregnancy, crash dieting and major stress can all cause shedding that looks similar. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Needham

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Topical minoxidil extends the growth phase and thickens individual hairs. The pairing is the standard. A compounded combination spray is another route, though it is not FDA-approved.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. It is FDA-approved for an enlarged prostate and used off-label for hair loss, most often in men who did not get enough from finasteride after a year. It lingers. Its half-life is about five weeks, so screening and side effect planning take more care. Minoxidil rounds it out.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Needham

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. The blend has no FDA approval. Women and finasteride-averse men use it most.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. Expect questions about family plans, libido concerns, mood history and pills versus topicals. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Women usually start with minoxidil.

    What to Expect Over Time in Needham

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Results last only as long as treatment does.
