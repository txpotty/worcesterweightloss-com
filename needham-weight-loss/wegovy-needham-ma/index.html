    What Wegovy Is in Needham

    Wegovy is brand-name semaglutide from Novo Nordisk, approved by the FDA specifically for weight loss. Each pen holds one weekly dose, with a hidden needle that pops out on press. No measuring is needed. Ozempic contains the same ingredient but is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes, so Wegovy is the correct prescription when weight management is the goal. There is also an FDA-approved oral Wegovy tablet for needle-free treatment.

    Who Wegovy Is For

    Adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with a weight-related condition, are the usual candidates. Qualifying conditions include high blood pressure, high cholesterol, prediabetes and sleep apnea. Teens can qualify too. The heart risk reduction label applies to adults with heart disease and a BMI of 27 or more. It is not for type 1 diabetes, and your provider weighs every factor against your full health history before writing a prescription.

    Wegovy Dosing Schedule in Needham

    Wegovy starts at 0.25 mg weekly and climbs through four more monthly steps until it reaches 2.4 mg. Expect about a month on each pen strength. Pick a weekday and keep it, morning or evening, fed or fasting. The pen goes under the skin of the belly or thigh, and moving the spot each week cuts down on redness and small bumps under the skin. A missed dose can be taken within five days, or skipped if the next one is under two days away.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Weeks one to four at 0.25 mg are a warm-up, and some people notice only a slightly smaller appetite. Nausea often shows up in the first two days after a shot. The 1 mg step often feels different. Around month three, portion sizes tend to drop on their own. In STEP 1, weight loss kept building through about week 60 before leveling off, so the first eight weeks are only a small part of the picture.

    Wegovy Side Effects in Needham

    Nausea, diarrhea, constipation and vomiting are the most reported side effects, and they tend to flare in the week after a step up. Smaller meals with less grease help a lot. Hydrate well. Less common risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, kidney problems from dehydration and low blood sugar when Wegovy is paired with insulin or a sulfonylurea. The label carries a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The intake asks about a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2, and either one means Wegovy is off the table. Your provider also looks for past pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, diabetic retinopathy, severe stomach emptying problems and any history of an eating disorder before writing a prescription. Pregnancy is a hard stop. Recent labs such as an A1c or kidney panel help with prior authorization.

    Wegovy Compared With Related Options in Needham

    Zepbound, the tirzepatide pen, produced larger average weight loss than Wegovy in the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, about 20 percent versus 14 percent at 72 weeks. Wegovy has the longer track record and the cardiovascular approval. Compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and comes in vials. Coverage often decides. Insurance rules usually narrow the choice quickly.
