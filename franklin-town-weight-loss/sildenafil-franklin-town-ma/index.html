    How Sildenafil Works in Franklin Town

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. That enzyme normally breaks down cGMP, the molecule that relaxes smooth muscle in the blood vessels of the penis, so blocking it lets blood flow in more easily during arousal. It does nothing without sexual stimulation. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. What it does is make an erection easier to reach and to hold.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. It works whether the cause is blood flow, nerves, stress or some mix. Men with diabetes or high blood pressure often respond well, though those conditions need their own care too. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. That is usually men who want a longer window.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Franklin Town

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Common strengths are 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, and most men start at 50 mg. One dose per 24 hours, maximum. A heavy or high-fat meal can delay it by an hour or so, which is why many men take it on a light stomach. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need several attempts before the timing clicks. Providers generally suggest trying a dose on four to eight separate occasions before calling it a failure, since nerves and alcohol can throw off the first attempt or two. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Keep a quick note of dose, timing and results. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Franklin Town

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. Some men notice a blue tint to their vision or extra light sensitivity for a few hours. Dizziness is more likely with alcohol, with blood pressure medication and in men who stand up quickly after taking a 100 mg dose. Sudden vision loss, sudden hearing loss or an erection lasting more than four hours needs emergency care. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. That includes nitroglycerin tablets or spray, isosorbide and recreational poppers, because the combination can drop blood pressure to dangerous levels. The pulmonary hypertension drug riociguat is excluded too. Your provider asks about heart attack or stroke in the past six months, unstable angina and severe liver or kidney disease. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Franklin Town

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, or can be taken every day at a low dose. Chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets are compounded formats, and compounded products are not FDA-approved. Generic sildenafil is FDA-approved and is chemically the same as Viagra, so it is often where providers start for men who want a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
