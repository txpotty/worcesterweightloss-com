    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Waltham, MA

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Waltham

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. DHT, made from testosterone, gradually miniaturizes follicles in genetically sensitive men, starting at the temples or crown. Finasteride lowers DHT, and minoxidil supports growth directly. Used together, they usually beat either one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Pattern loss is the usual reason, though low ferritin, thyroid trouble and postpartum shedding can mimic it. Topical minoxidil is the approved starting point. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Waltham

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Minoxidil, FDA-approved as a topical, keeps follicles in the growth phase longer so strands come in thicker. The pairing is the standard. Some men use a compounded topical spray that combines both, which is not FDA-approved but may reduce whole-body exposure to finasteride.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. It is FDA-approved for an enlarged prostate and used off-label for hair loss, most often in men who did not get enough from finasteride after a year. It lingers. Its half-life is about five weeks, so screening and side effect planning take more care. Minoxidil rounds it out.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Waltham

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. The blend has no FDA approval. Women and finasteride-averse men use it most.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. Expect questions about family plans, libido concerns, mood history and pills versus topicals. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Women usually start with minoxidil.

    What to Expect Over Time in Waltham

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. That is why most treatments take three to six months to show a visible change and around twelve months to reach their full effect, even when they are working from the first week. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Stopping treatment lets the loss resume.
