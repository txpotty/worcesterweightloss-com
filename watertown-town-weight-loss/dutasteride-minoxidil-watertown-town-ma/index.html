    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Watertown Town

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Blocking both enzymes cuts DHT further than finasteride can. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil works on growth, not hormones. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. A year of steady finasteride with photos is the usual bar before stepping up. Some men with aggressive early loss discuss it sooner. It is not used in women who could become pregnant.

    Dosing and Schedule in Watertown Town

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Because it has a half-life of about five weeks, some providers prescribe it daily while others use a lower or less frequent schedule, such as a few times a week, to balance effect and side effects. Minoxidil goes on topically once or twice a day. Oral minoxidil is off-label.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. The early shed is normal and usually fades. By months three to six, many men notice less loss and some thickening. Comparison trials favored dutasteride over finasteride for hair counts. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Watertown Town

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. The long half-life means side effects can take weeks to months to clear. Mood changes should be reported right away. Scalp itch is the usual topical issue. Oral minoxidil may cause body hair or swelling.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. Dutasteride lowers PSA by about half, so any doctor screening your prostate needs to know you take it and will usually double the reading to interpret it.
