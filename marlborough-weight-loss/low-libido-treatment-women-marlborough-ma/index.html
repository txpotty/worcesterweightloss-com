    What Causes Low Libido in Marlborough

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Common contributors include SSRIs and SNRIs, hormonal birth control, perimenopause, thyroid problems, chronic stress, poor sleep, pain during sex and relationship strain. So does how you feel in your body. Persistent, distressing low desire without another explanation is called hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. It often helps women whose interest changed after starting a medication, having a baby, entering perimenopause or going through a stressful stretch at work or home. You can start without a label. If lower desire does not bother you, there is nothing to fix, because distress is part of the definition. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Marlborough

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. Bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and also called PT-141, is a 1.75 mg injection under the skin used at least 45 minutes before sex, no more than once a day or eight times a month. Oxytocin is compounded. It comes as a nasal spray or troche before intimacy, used off-label and without FDA approval. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 effects show up within hours. Flibanserin takes four to eight weeks, and it is typically stopped at the eight week mark if there is no benefit. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Marlborough

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Those effects are worse with alcohol or CYP3A4-blocking drugs, which is why the dose goes in at bedtime and the label limits drinking near that dose. Nausea is common with PT-141. It can also cause flushing, headache, injection site reactions, a brief rise in blood pressure and darker skin patches with repeated use. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. Drugs like fluconazole, clarithromycin and certain HIV medications rule it out, and drinking has to be kept away from the bedtime dose. Blood pressure matters for PT-141. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Marlborough

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It fits women who drink little and like a routine, with clinical trials showing a modest average gain of about one more satisfying encounter a month versus placebo. PT-141 is as-needed. That suits some women, but nausea puts others off. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
