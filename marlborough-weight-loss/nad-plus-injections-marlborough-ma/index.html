    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Marlborough, MA

    How NAD+ Works in Marlborough

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Older tissue tends to have less. Injected under the skin, NAD+ skips digestion entirely, but researchers are still working out how much of it reaches muscle, brain and other tissue.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. Some add NAD+ during GLP-1 weight loss to support how they feel while eating less. NAD+ does not treat disease or cause weight loss, and pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or serious liver or kidney disease usually rule it out.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Marlborough

    Small shots, slow push. Many compounded protocols begin around 50 milligrams two or three times a week and rise gradually, often toward 100 milligrams or more, as tolerance builds. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. Pushing the plunger over about a minute cuts down on flushing.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. A wave of warmth, some queasiness or brief chest tightness right after the shot is common, and it typically passes in a few minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Marlborough

    Flushing leads the list. Injection site redness, stinging and soreness, along with nausea, lightheadedness and headache, are also common. Too much too quickly can cause chest pressure, cramping or palpitations, which typically ease with a smaller dose and a slower push. Rare allergic reactions need emergency care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and supplements first. You will be asked about pregnancy, cancer history, liver and kidney health, heart conditions and any NMN, nicotinamide riboside or niacin you already take. Labs are not always required. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Marlborough

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. An NAD+ drip delivers a larger amount in one sitting but can take two to four hours, and many people feel more nausea and cramping during the infusion. NAD+ nasal spray avoids needles at a smaller dose. Oral precursors like NMN and nicotinamide riboside are supplements that help the body make its own NAD+.
