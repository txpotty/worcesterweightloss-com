    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Framingham, MA

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Framingham

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Because it hits both, it lowers DHT in the blood by more than 90 percent, compared with roughly 70 percent for finasteride, which blocks only type 2. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil works on growth, not hormones. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. A year of steady finasteride with photos is the usual bar before stepping up. Some men with aggressive early loss discuss it sooner. It is not used in women who could become pregnant.

    Dosing and Schedule in Framingham

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil goes on topically once or twice a day. Low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label alternative.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. That early shed is common, and it typically settles by month three as follicles move into a new growth phase. Around month six, density often improves. In head-to-head studies, dutasteride 0.5 mg produced greater increases in hair count than finasteride 1 mg over six months, which is why it is the common step up. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Framingham

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. Breast tenderness or enlargement is rare, and because the drug lingers for months, side effects may take longer to fade after stopping than they would with finasteride. Report low mood promptly. Minoxidil can irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil may cause body hair or swelling.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. PSA numbers drop by roughly half, which your other doctors should factor in. Blood donation is paused for six months after stopping. Pregnant partners should not touch leaking capsules.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Framingham
