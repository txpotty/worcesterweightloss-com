    How Glutathione Injections Work in Lexington, MA

    How Glutathione Works in Lexington

    Glutathione is made from glutamate, cysteine and glycine. The liver produces the most, and the molecule neutralizes free radicals and regenerates vitamins C and E so they can keep working. Detox pathways depend on it. Levels can fall with age, illness and chronic stress, and injections deliver glutathione directly into tissue, avoiding the gut breakdown that limits most oral forms.

    Who Glutathione Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want antioxidant support or better recovery. People on GLP-1 medication or NAD+ sometimes add glutathione, but it has no effect on body weight and is not a treatment for a diagnosed disease. Skin lightening is excluded. People who are pregnant, breastfeeding, allergic or have unstable asthma are generally not candidates.

    Glutathione Dosing and Schedule in Lexington

    One to three injections a week. A common range is a few hundred milligrams per dose, injected into muscle or subcutaneously, with the route chosen by your provider. Refrigerate and protect from light. You will receive syringes, alcohol pads and written instructions for drawing up and injecting each dose.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Early changes are rare, and by weeks two to four some people describe better recovery after exercise, though many feel nothing specific. Glutathione supports processes you cannot feel. Check-ins review soreness, stomach symptoms and dosing.

    Glutathione Side Effects in Lexington

    Injection site pain leads the list. Redness and bruising where you inject are common, along with occasional mild nausea, abdominal cramping, bloating or a temporary headache. Rash or itching can signal an allergy, and because inhaled glutathione has triggered airway tightening in asthma, your provider is especially cautious with breathing history. Wheezing needs immediate care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and asthma status. Expect questions on pregnancy, asthma control, allergies, organ health and current cancer treatment, which can be affected by antioxidants. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if available.

    Glutathione Injections Compared to Other Options in Lexington

    IV drips are the usual comparison. Glutathione IV pushes deliver larger amounts in a clinic, while at-home injections are quicker and do not require an appointment. Liposomal forms may do slightly better. NAC supplies the building block cysteine, while NAD+ shots are aimed at energy production instead of antioxidant activity.
